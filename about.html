About GMS Solutions

Turning Geological Knowledge into Resource Opportunities.

GMS Solutions (The Geo Masters) is a multidisciplinary geological exploration and mining consultancy based in Hyderabad, India. We combine field expertise, scientific methods and modern geospatial technologies to support mineral exploration and responsible development.

Our approach integrates geological investigation, geophysical surveys, drilling, sampling, resource assessment and technical reporting to support informed decisions across the mineral value chain.

  • Integrated geology, geophysics and mining expertise
  • Field-oriented, data-driven project execution
  • Technical reporting aligned with applicable frameworks
  • Commitment to responsible and sustainable development
